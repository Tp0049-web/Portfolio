Whistler-Wave Plasma Instrumentation — SPACE 582

Fall 2026 · LANL-sponsored

Graduate space systems design project sponsored by Los Alamos National Laboratory, centered on generating VLF plasma waves in space via a compact, modulated electron gun.

  • Working within a mission concept addressing the engineering challenge of radiating waves whose wavelength exceeds 1 km using conventional antennas.
  • Evaluating a compact electron gun and modulator system designed to generate 1 µs modulated pulses as a compact alternative to large deployable antennas.
  • Project scope includes electron source design (gridded gun vs. mod-anode configurations), shock/vibration qualification of cathode hardware, and integration with a high-voltage power supply and modulator.
